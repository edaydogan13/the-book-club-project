# Books & Friends — Six readers. One book a month.

We are a small circle of six readers across Istanbul, Dubai, NYC, and San Francisco who read one book every month together, prioritizing depth over velocity.

## Now Reading: Born a Crime by Trevor Noah
The compelling, inspiring, and comically inventive story of a young man coming of age in South Africa during the twilight of apartheid and the tumultuous days of freedom that followed.
